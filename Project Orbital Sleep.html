    NASA data used

    The CO₂ alert levels in the sleep quarters panel come from NASA. The crew members and their sleep values are simulated.

    CO₂ limit on the space station: 3 mmHg, averaged over one hour. NASA reports decreased sleep, fatigue and nausea above 4.5 mmHg, so this page shows Watch above 3 mmHg and Act above 4.5 mmHg.

    Source: NASA, OCHMO-TB-004 Carbon Dioxide
